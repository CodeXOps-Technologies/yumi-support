Theme Overview

About YUMI

YUMI is a versatile Shopify theme designed to adapt to your brand's style and scale. While it comes with a clean black-and-white base to get started quickly, every color and visual element can be customized to suit your store's personality.

Built for mid to large product catalogs, YUMI focuses on structure, performance, and a visually rich layout that helps stores with extensive product ranges showcase their collections in an engaging and user-friendly way.

Quick Start Guide

1

Download Theme

Download the YUMI theme ZIP file from your purchase confirmation email or download page.

2

Upload to Shopify

Log in to your Shopify admin → Online Store → Themes → Add theme → Upload ZIP file

3

Customize

Click "Customize" to open the theme editor and start personalizing your store

4

Publish

When ready, click "Publish" to make YUMI your live theme

⚠️ Before Publishing: Always preview your theme thoroughly on different devices before making it live. Use the preview function to test all pages.
